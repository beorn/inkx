The bottleneck isn't AI capability.
It's the workspace.

Context is fragmented

Calendar here, notes there, tasks elsewhere. Your knowledge is scattered across a dozen apps.

Agents are blind

They only see what you paste in. No persistent memory. No shared workspace.

No history

What did the agent do while you weren't looking? No audit trail. No undo.

No "run" mode

Agents can't work in the background. They need you staring at them.

Features

One workspace.
Infinite possibilities.

Knowledge Base
Notes
Tasks
Calendar
Contacts

Unified Knowledge Tree

Notes, tasks, calendar, contacts — all nodes in one tree. Your markdown becomes a living knowledge base with bidirectional links and full-text search.

  • Obsidian-compatible markdown
  • Wikilinks & backlinks
  • CalDAV/CardDAV sync

Event-Sourced

Every change logged. Undo anything. Replay history. Full audit trail for you and your agents.

Local-First

Your data stays on your machine. Sync via Git. No vendor lock-in. Export anytime.

Watch Mode

Edit in VS Code, Obsidian, or vim. The index updates in real-time as you work.

Agent Teams

Spawn agents, assign work, watch progress. Pure function agents you can undo and replay.

Like Linear or Asana, but local-first and markdown-based. Agents do the grunt work while you focus on decisions.

Personal Knowledge

Like Notion or Apple Notes, but local-first with agents integrated. Your second brain that actually works for you.
