# [x] My Tasks board is flat — missing section grouping from Asana @km/tui #bug #P2

The 'My Tasks' view for users shows a flat list of tasks without section grouping. In Asana, My Tasks has sections (e.g., Recently Assigned, Today, Upcoming) that group tasks. The km import doesn't preserve this section structure, or the board view doesn't render it. Tasks should be grouped by their section within the My Tasks project.
