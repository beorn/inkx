# [x] Card view: hide attachments (collapse by default) @km/tui #feature #P1 @claude:23485adf

Attachments (images, files) inside cards should not display at the card level in board view. They should be collapsed/hidden by default and only visible when expanding the card (detail pane or zoomed view). Currently they take up visual space without being actionable.
